# 100 ideas

★ = my top picks.

## Flight and vehicles

1. ★ **Paper plane:** no engine, pure glide. Gain altitude from updrafts off hills (Twin Peaks, Bernal, Telegraph) and warm rooftops. Hardest and most beautiful mode.
2. ★ **Quadcopter drone:** hover, strafe, precision. Battery drains, rooftop landing pads recharge it.
3. **UFO:** tractor beam picks up collectibles, and can gently abduct a Waymo for a bonus.
4. **Seagull:** unlocked at Pier 39. Dive-bomb tourists for fries (fries = currency).
5. **Karl the Fog:** final unlockable. Slow, huge, everything you drift over goes gray and spooky.
6. **Blue Angels wingman:** Fleet Week vehicle, fly formation over the Marina.
7. **Hang glider at Fort Funston:** ride the cliff updraft next to the real hang gliders.
8. **Real wind:** live SF wind. Afternoon westerlies off Ocean Beach push you east; the paper plane has to use them.
9. ★ **Real fog layer:** the marine layer rolls in late afternoon, visibility drops inside it, some collectibles only show above or below it.
10. **Altitude bands:** street level (alleys, murals, Muni wires), rooftop, skyline. Different collectibles in each band.
11. **Upgrade tree:** battery, speed, fog lights, quiet props (do not spook the parrots), bigger tractor beam.
12. **SFO airspace:** climb too high near the airport and a "you are in Class B airspace" warning starts a chase.

## Landmarks and SF collectibles

13. ★ **Golden Gate Bridge:** fly under the deck, collect an International Orange paint chip.
14. **Alcatraz:** thread through the cell house, escape-room clue inside.
15. **Painted Ladies:** a Full House style music sting as you fly past.
16. **Lombard Street:** slalom down the crooked block.
17. **Coit Tower** and the wild parrots of Telegraph Hill. The flock follows you for a minute.
18. **Sutro Tower:** climb to the tip. Its "broadcast" is a radio channel of clues.
19. **Salesforce Tower crown** at night, plus Salesforce Park, the garden on top of the Transit Center.
20. **Transamerica Pyramid:** thread the spire.
21. **Palace of Fine Arts:** fly through the rotunda.
22. **Ferry Building:** clock tower, Saturday farmers market collectibles (oysters, sourdough).
23. **Golden Gate Park:** bison paddock, Japanese Tea Garden, Stow Lake, de Young tower, Cal Academy living roof, the Dutch windmills.
24. **Dolores Park:** crowd density follows the real day of week and weather.
25. **Twin Peaks:** panorama checkpoint, the one spot where you see the whole map.
26. **Lands End:** Sutro Baths ruins, the labyrinth, shipwreck remains at low tide.
27. **Oracle Park** and the McCovey Cove kayakers.
28. ★ **In-N-Out at Fisherman's Wharf** (the only one in SF). Secret menu collectibles: Animal Style, 4x4, and the Flying Dutchman, which might as well have been named for this game.
29. **Food trail:** Mission burrito (La Taqueria vs El Farolito, player vote), Boudin sourdough, Ghirardelli, Tartine, Chinatown dim sum.
30. **Hidden SF:** Wave Organ, Seward Street slides, 16th Ave Tiled Steps, Filbert Steps, Lyon Street Steps.
31. **Musee Mecanique:** the real antique arcade at Pier 45, playable inside the game.
32. **Fort Mason:** the piers, Founders Inc as a collectible, Off the Grid food trucks on Friday nights.

## Tech and startup layer

33. ★ **Company cards:** YC, a16z, Stripe, Corgi, Founders Inc and more. Each card has founding year, origin story, first product, one fun fact.
34. **Startup origin trail:** buildings where famous companies started (South Park, SoMa lofts) shown as glowing "genesis" markers.
35. ★ **Commit Towers:** fake skyscrapers whose height is the public GitHub commit volume of SF-based orgs. They grow live.
36. **Commit fireflies:** commits stream out of rooftops as particles you fly through and collect.
37. **GitHub's former SF HQ:** easter egg for its famous Oval Office replica lobby.
38. **Hackathon beacons:** this weekend's real SF hackathons (public listings) light up their venues. Fly in, see pixel hackers typing.
39. **Hacker NPCs:** archetypes, never real people. Patagonia-vest VC, stealth founder, intern with three laptops.
40. **Coffee = fuel:** Philz, Blue Bottle, Sightglass recharge you.
41. **Pitch rooftop:** land on a VC building, give a 30-second pitch (multiple choice or LLM-judged), win a term sheet.
42. **Demo Day:** boss event where every startup you collected presents. Your collection decides the outcome.
43. **Waymo swarm:** driverless cars on real streets. Follow one to its depot to find a secret.
44. **101 billboard row:** collect the cryptic AI billboards. A parody generator writes new ones.
45. **Startup graveyard:** at night, ghosts of dead SF startups (Juicero and friends) haunt their old offices and hand out lore.
46. **Unicorn hunt:** hidden unicorn sprites near unicorn HQs; decacorns get a golden one.
47. **Sand Hill Road expansion:** fly down the Peninsula to the real VC strip.
48. **Tech shuttle chase:** catch a private shuttle along its route.
49. **AI lab row:** labs as landmark buildings with lore cards, parody names optional.
50. **Hacker News overlay:** SF companies on today's HN front page glow.

## Clues, story and quests

51. ★ **Main quest, "The Lost Commit":** someone force-pushed over SF's history. Fragments hidden at landmarks reassemble it.
52. **Clue chain:** each collectible gives a fragment; five fragments solve a riddle that points to the next spot.
53. ★ **Emperor Norton as your guide:** the real 1859 SF eccentric who declared himself Emperor of the United States.
54. ★ **Time travel lens:** 1849 Gold Rush, 1906 earthquake, 1967 Summer of Love, 1999 dot-com, today's AI boom. Same map, different era.
55. ★ **Buried ships:** x-ray mode reveals the Gold Rush ships under the Financial District landfill (real).
56. **1906 ghost mode:** fly over the burned city, find what survived.
57. ★ **Daily clue:** one Wordle-style location riddle per day with a shareable result grid.
58. **Seasonal events:** Fleet Week, Bay to Breakers costumes, Outside Lands, Dreamforce taking over SoMa, Lunar New Year dragon, Hardly Strictly Bluegrass.
59. **Factions:** Builders, Investors, Locals. Your pick changes clues and the ending.
60. **Branching endings:** who restores SF's history decides what the final skyline looks like.
61. **Bay Bridge Troll:** find the ironworkers' legendary troll (real bridge lore).
62. **Neighborhood chapters:** each neighborhood is a chapter with its own mini-story.
63. **Secret rooftop rooms:** only reachable by threading narrow gaps.
64. **IRL bonus:** if your phone is physically near a landmark, unlock a real-world-only card.
